## New angle: delayed-CC timing is itself a graded decision, not a binary immediate-vs-wait choice

H-C-02 (RETAIN) already establishes that immediate CC after every assignment is
rejected. It does not, on its own, specify *how* delayed the CC decision should be —
"delayed" could mean anything from "wait one day" to "wait until fully recovered to
basis." This is the same open-parameter structure as H-A-02/H-A-04's
bound-must-exist-vs-which-bound split, applied to the CC timing decision specifically
rather than the stock-exit decision. **Recommendation (not yet a registered
hypothesis):** a future session should register an H-C-03, structured exactly like
H-A-04 (pre-registered sweep of candidate CC-delay triggers, selected via purged
walk-forward, confirmed on untouched OOS), rather than treating "delayed" as a single
implicit policy. Not registered as a full hypothesis in this pass, to avoid adding an
incomplete/under-specified entry to the canonical registry — flagged here for a future
session to do properly, consistent with Part E's "don't implement/specify just to have
something to point at."
