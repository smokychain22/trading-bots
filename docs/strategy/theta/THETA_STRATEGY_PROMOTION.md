# Strategy promotion

A branch starts unvalidated and non-executable. Promotion requires point-in-time data, resolved whole chains, direction-aware costs and fills, immutable dataset and model hashes, untouched final OOS, sufficient independent N, calibration, ablation improvement, regime stability, positive after-cost EV, acceptable PF and drawdown, ES/CVaR, capital-day efficiency, and no catastrophic subgroup failure.

Eligible research status does not automatically activate Production. Operator governance and all execution gates still apply. Drift hooks must cover EV, calibration, tail risk, drawdown, slippage, feature distributions, regimes, and sample confidence. Allowed states are research-only, challenger or shadow, champion, degraded, reduced, hold-only, and retired, with explicit evidence for every transition.
