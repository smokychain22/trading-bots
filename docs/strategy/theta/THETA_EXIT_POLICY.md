# Exit policy

Research baselines include fixed 25, 50, and 75 percent premium capture, time and DTE exits, dynamic remaining EV, dynamic EV plus hard risk, and dynamic EV plus flow invalidation. None is promoted.

The Production question is whether HOLD has more after-cost value than CLOSE after tail risk, capital time, opportunity cost, execution cost, and uncertainty. Fixed profit or loss percentages remain challengers until complete-chain, independent OOS evidence supports them.
