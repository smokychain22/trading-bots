# Covered-call policy

Covered-call candidates require confirmed stock coverage and compare premium, retained upside, desired exit, whole-chain stock basis, call-away consequences, event and ex-dividend risk, execution quality, and capital time. Optionomics context can rank a candidate but cannot provide the order price.

SELL_CC remains a lifecycle action. It is not a generic income overlay and is never automatic after assignment. CLOSE_CC, ROLL_CC, ALLOW_CALL_AWAY, and continued recovery must remain visible alternatives.
