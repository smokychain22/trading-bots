# Management policy

Management enumerates the complete state-appropriate frontier under one FusionSnapshot. CSP compares HOLD, CLOSE_FULL, ROLL, LET_EXPIRE, ACCEPT_ASSIGNMENT, and REDEPLOY. Assigned stock compares RECOVERY_WAIT, SELL_STOCK, and SELL_CC. A covered call compares HOLD_CC, CLOSE_CC, ROLL_CC, and ALLOW_CALL_AWAY.

The future action utility compares remaining after-cost value, tail risk, incremental capital days, execution cost, opportunity cost, inventory consequences, and uncertainty. No action wins while required components are unknown. Current behavior selects the safe no-order action and records all alternatives.
