# Roll policy

A roll is a BUY_TO_CLOSE of the old exposure plus a separately identified opening leg. The old realized P&L is immutable and both legs retain lineage. A net credit is cash-flow description, not proof that rolling improves economics.

ROLL must compete with HOLD, CLOSE, assignment, and close plus redeploy under the same snapshot. It remains blocked while incremental after-cost value, tail risk, capital days, and execution costs are unknown. The atomic lifecycle writer validates multiplier-aware economics and preserves both leg IDs.
