# THETA strategy constitution

THETA maximizes complete lifecycle, after-cost wealth per unit of capital time while controlling tail and assigned-inventory risk. Managed Episode win rate is a diagnostic and cohort research target. It is never the optimization target or a promised result.

The authoritative lifecycle is CASH to CSP to close, expire, roll, or assignment. Assignment creates stock inventory. Stock enters recovery evaluation, where RECOVERY_WAIT, SELL_STOCK, and SELL_CC compete. Covered calls can close, expire, roll, or call stock away. Every old realized loss remains immutable.

Hard safety can veto any model. Quantity zero, WAIT, and RECOVERY_WAIT are valid. Delta is a Greek, not win probability. Optionomics supplies research context, while Alpaca supplies executable and broker truth. Missing values stay UNKNOWN.
