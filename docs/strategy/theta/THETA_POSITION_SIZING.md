# Position sizing

Quantity is the floor of the minimum feasible broker, collateral, assignment, tail or stress, concentration, and portfolio capacity. AEGIS can reduce that result or set it to zero. Tightening a capacity cannot increase quantity.

Quantity zero is valid. Missing buying power, required collateral, or multiplier makes sizing non-executable. No loss streak, model confidence, or target win rate can force quantity to one or increase size after a loss.
