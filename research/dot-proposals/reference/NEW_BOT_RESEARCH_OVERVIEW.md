# New-bot research overview — 9 October 2026

## Result and evidence boundary

Three executable offline prototypes and full future-bot playbooks have passed bounded independent structural review. All financial fixtures are invented. There are zero qualified historical option episodes, zero qualified live capsules and zero actual fills. After-cost out-of-sample expectancy, win rate, capacity, practical tail adequacy and deployment readiness remain unestablished. The code can reject malformed or unsupported evidence; field-level checks cannot authenticate a data provider, calendar, legal entitlement, inventory or account attestation.

This package preserves the reviewed final files and correction trails that are safe to distribute. It neither changes the existing THETA production registry nor modifies or replaces the separate original H proposal. It does not enable a data collector, paper orders, live orders or a recurring monitor.

## Gamma Trader

`gamma/PLAYBOOK_GAMMA_RESEARCH_V1.md` defines two intraday long-option hypotheses: positive modeled-gamma wall mean reversion and negative modeled-gamma confirmed breakout. Independent price/VWAP/realized-volatility benchmarks, gate-only and wall ablations, matched price-only exits, optional gamma-exit challengers, quote/cost lineage, contract-identity locking, causal bars and censored exits are explicit.

Open interest is not signed dealer inventory. Inventory scenarios are explicitly injected model inputs; neither dealer books nor actual hedge flows are observed. Vanna/charm remains deferred with no signal. A modeled opportunity's hypothetical option P&L is not a feasible overlapping portfolio result. Missing coverage cannot become a neutral gamma regime. No provider adapter or market observation collector exists in this prototype.

The independent review records 62 developer and 33 independent test methods. The package contains their final receipt and deterministic synthetic outputs; rerunning them does not create new independent evidence.

## Volatility Arbitrageur

`volatility/PLAYBOOKS_v1.md` defines matched-target variance/realized-QV screening, same-expiry credit-spread and condor research nominations, five comparison arms, explicit cost reserves and conservative package, assignment-funding, delta, vega and gamma limits. ACT365 total-variance clocks and exact target matching are required; equal-session mean annual variance and pooled elapsed-time variance are deliberately separate estimands. Vega is dollars per one volatility point; gamma risk uses a second-order one-percent move approximation.

Term, skew and event variants remain diagnostic or deferred. ATM/model IV is not model-free tradable variance. Intact terminal spread/condor loss does not bound all asymmetric exercise, stock inventory, financing or hedge-removal paths. All option legs being flat is separate from authenticated capital release: an explicit supplied reserve-release attestation is required and cannot override residual exposure or uncertainty. EV and profit probability remain unknown.

The current review records 70 developer and 33 independent test methods. The older 101-method review is explicitly superseded by the final 103-method receipt after reserve-release tightening. Eight invented scenarios generate 80 nomination rows; those rows are not market episodes or independent samples.

## Theta Harvester

`harvester/STRATEGY_CONTRACT_v0.1.1.md` defines CSP, put-credit-spread and WAIT comparisons on the same initial cash, with separately enforced tail-loss and gross assignment-funding caps. It records clock/security identity, provenance, event coverage, quote authority, realistic partial-management states, original-credit denominators and no automatic capital release. Its daily-return EWMA volatility uses a 65-session input window and is annualized using 252 trading sessions per year. The comparison with 30-calendar-day ATM IV remains an unmatched screening proxy, not a matched variance-price estimator.

The common-cash fixed-reserve expiry scenarios do not implement the intended full early-close, assignment and stock-inventory common-horizon replay. Equal starting cash does not equalize tail loss, delta, short notional or delivery financing. A canonical annualized ceiling is not an annualized realized return. The frozen public pure arithmetic module is only a reference dependency; future approved integration would import the actual canonical module.

Final v0.1.1 passes 94 developer and 19 independent reviewer methods. One independent method contains 200 seeded randomized cash-ledger cases; the author grid contains 310 invented payoff rows and 620 arithmetic checks. These units overlap and must not be added into a statistical sample. Nine independently identified defects were fixed across the preserved review history. Earlier passing suites did not establish correctness.

## Cross-family use and next evidence

Gamma per-opportunity hypothetical dollars, Volatility risk-budget package diagnostics and Harvester same-cash expiry ceilings have different horizons, opportunities and estimands. They do not support a common leaderboard or a best-bot conclusion. A defensible later comparison needs a common admissible opportunity set, a feasible portfolio ledger, clocks, costs, cash yield, assignment/funding policy and qualified outcomes.

The next useful step is a separately authorized, read-only point-in-time coverage assessment from a lawful free or already-entitled source. Required fields include source and first-known timestamps, original revision identity, entitlement, security/deliverable identity, session/calendar lineage, quote bid/ask/size, costs, model/forecast vintage and outcome completeness. Unknowns remain explicit rejection or WAIT records. Session analytics, historical requests carrying current regime labels and modified indicative prices cannot certify point-in-time executable BBO. No paid data access or account entitlement is inferred from public documentation.

A scheduled market opening alone establishes neither usable data nor strategy readiness. Until qualified observations exist, retain DATA_NOT_AVAILABLE and these offline evidence limits. Passing tests does not justify a 70–80% win-rate claim or any other performance target.

## Reproduction and integrity

See `README.md` for the three entrypoints and existing-runtime requirements. `MANIFEST_SHA256.json` binds exact package file bytes. A separate packaging receipt binds the ZIP hash and records one run of each entrypoint from a clean extraction. Original frozen author files and counts stay separate from regenerated packaging receipts. No production tests, historical backtests, external API calls or statistical studies are added by that packaging run.
