# H narrowing: calendar clocks are a required attribution layer

Research-only finding, 2026-10-08. Baseline H entry eligibility 2–5 DTE versus proposed 3–5 DTE. No change to production authority, risk thresholds, no-roll rules, or mandatory-close precedence. The distinct net-50 primary/net-80 secondary exit study stays separate. No market observations were obtained; the reported 78 synthetic passes remain engineering evidence only. No prior 35-check or 78-check suite was rerun.

## Finding

Excluding 2 calendar-DTE can change entry weekdays, eligible management sessions, closure gaps, scheduled-event exposure, and capital duration simultaneously. It does not mechanically remove weekends: a Friday-to-Monday 3-calendar-DTE candidate can remain eligible while a Monday-to-Wednesday 2-DTE candidate disappears. Conversely, a Friday-expiry-only universe can turn the rule into an entry-weekday filter. These are calendar examples, not observed trades. The direction and magnitude depend on the actual expiration universe, selector, and mandatory management horizon. Therefore a profitable aggregate narrowing would establish a policy difference, not a pure causal DTE effect.

## Incremental comparison and falsification

Primary policy estimand: on the same opportunity stream and fixed initial available capital, compare the unchanged 2–5 selector with its precisely specified 3–5 restriction; freeze management rules. Report common unchanged selections, removed 2-DTE selections, replacements, and cash/no-trade opportunities. Any net-50/net-80 comparison uses separately named outputs and does not explain the DTE effect.

Predeclare exposure strata from entry-known information: underlying/contract class, entry weekday and decision window, eligible session count, closure pattern (none/ordinary overnight/weekend/holiday), known-event type/window, and existing moneyness/liquidity strata. Include continuous calendar/eligible minutes and prospective capital-hours. Show empty cells/common support explicitly. Exact matching every clock may make DTE deterministic and erase overlap; in that case a pure DTE effect is not identifiable. Do not manufacture overlap through regression extrapolation. Realized holding duration and exits are outcomes/mediators, not entry matching covariates.

Sharp hypothesis H-clock: any apparent aggregate improvement from excluding 2-DTE is an exposure-composition effect; on genuine common support, its standardized incremental net capital-hour reward is nonpositive. A prospective test can falsify this if the clock-standardized increment is positive with a predeclared date-clustered confidence interval excluding zero, alongside unchanged existing tail/feasibility criteria. Cluster overlapping positions by date/event rather than treating all contracts as independent. Register the inference rule before outcomes; this is a research criterion, not a new trading risk limit.

Reject an H-narrowing economic-advantage claim if the held-out policy increment is nonpositive after executable costs and idle-cash opportunities; reject a *pure DTE* interpretation if the advantage vanishes/reverses under common-support standardization or exists only in changed weekend/event strata. Insufficient support, missing event vintage, unverified execution/capital release, or zero market observations means INCONCLUSIVE, not a profitable or disproved strategy. An effect confined to an event stratum can justify a new hypothesis, not post hoc threshold tuning or activation.

Exact accounting diagnostic, independently derived: for disjoint realized paths with P_j net dollars and C_j capital-hours, pooled reward = sum(P_j)/sum(C_j) = sum[w_j × (P_j/C_j)], w_j=C_j/sum(C_j). Consequently aggregate capital efficiency can improve solely because exposure weights change. For nonoverlapping identified stratum means, difference = sum[w_base × (r_narrow−r_base)] + sum[(w_narrow−w_base) × r_narrow]. The first term standardizes exposure weights; the second is composition. This is accounting, not causality. Cash opportunities need a fixed opportunity-budget denominator too, so a strategy cannot look superior merely by deleting low-return trades or declaring idle capital free.

## Minimum next evidence / stopping condition

The next useful increment is an entry-clock ledger reusing existing normalization, attached to the first lawful, fresh, executable paired market observations, followed by exposure balance/common-support reporting. Existing synthetic passes cannot fill those observations. Stop the present bounded research here: three new sources and a prospective design are complete; no price sample was acquired, no P&L calculated, and no live regime or production recommendation is made.
